# ADR-017 — Separate synthetic staff control and durable provisioning

Accepted bounded MOD-A-001/006 implementation boundary on 2026-10-10 after independent security review and disposable PostgreSQL verification. Frozen API bodies remain unchanged; production identity/onboarding, unattended control-job execution and commercial provider activation remain gated.

The internal `control-plane-port.ts` and closed `synthetic-staff-v1.schema.json` are committed before consumers. Forward migration0012 introduces FORCE-RLS staff sessions, control Jobs and durable control idempotency. Distinct non-owner/NOSUPERUSER/NOBYPASSRLS/NOINHERIT control and identity-issuer roles have no passwords in SQL. Runtime/worker/control cannot reach issuer; control cannot reach runtime/worker, and reverse control crossings deny. Startup must check both expected current/session login and recursive role membership, including SET ROLE paths with NOINHERIT. The issuer alone inserts an opaque session after verified OIDC and current principal resolution. Control cannot mint sessions from a forged actor GUC.

Control authorization requires the current opaque hash, active staff principal, session provenance/expiry and current catalog platform permission. Missing/forged context cannot read identity/principal directories. The only identity projection is the server-approved synthetic owner fixture with provision permission; email/phone are unselectable. Principal and owner identity SHARE locks remain until transaction commit; row-version UPDATE privileges exist only to enable locking, and WITH CHECK(false) denies identity/principal writes.

Frozen organization creation returns one durable queued Job and provisioning organization, with current-authority idempotent replay. The initial local bootstrap policy binds the existing verified synthetic owner, synthetic-starter monthly plan, CRM/billing capabilities and no charges. Owner identity/version and policy are rechecked on execution. SaaS, CRM and Billing each own transaction-bound bootstrap ports. One claimed execution transaction inserts built-in presets, a non-staff Owner, business profile, six stages, default website source/routing and mock billing defaults; activation and successful Job commit together. Failure rolls back defaults and leaves the organization provisioning, with a separate safe failure ledger transaction. Claims bind the exact requester/Job/UUID/current lease. Existing active/suspended/archived organizations cannot receive provisioning Jobs/defaults, completed Jobs cannot reopen, and activation immediately removes seed authority.

Initial support grants contain a reason, synthetic provenance, active recipient and at most one hour of database-clock expiry. The only customer permissions are contacts/leads/tasks/deals/pipeline/sources/tags read; related-source permissions and current CRM capability remain required. Grants never establish tenant membership or authorize writes, notes, exports, consent, billing or provider effects. Current session/principal/selected grant locks and expiry checks precede and follow projection. Exact replay preserves the original result without renewal. Revocation is irreversible. Customer reads use a CRM-owned `SupportCrmReadPort` and safe audit references, with an explicit purpose/expiry staff banner; control credentials alone cannot bypass selected-grant policies.

Local staff session/logout, support selection/clear/revoke and requester Job execution/read routes live under `/dev/staff/` with explicitly enabled synthetic mode, actual loopback, Origin and mutation CSRF/idempotency/revision guards. Separate HttpOnly staff/member cookies never combine authority. All responses remain private/no-store. Frozen platform operations may register only their published request/response bodies; missing production staff Session/Job/revoke/onboarding contracts stay explicit gaps. The root router/BFF remains the integration owner; consumers can first run behind unregistered adapters in disposable tests.

Pre-consumer evidence:15/15 real-login database probe groups plus12 direct/multi-hop role-crossing checks, checksum replay, three new FORCE-RLS tables, forged directory denial, expired/revoked support and principal locks, active-target/after-activation seed denial, current claim/lease takeover, owner version/verified-flag races and transaction rollback. Raw proposal evidence is retained in `apps/api/proposals`; the actual migration differs only in its reviewed header. Application HTTP/concurrency/bootstrap/UI acceptance is still required before claiming the slice implemented. No live identity, payments, messages, Trestle access or production rollout follows from this decision.
