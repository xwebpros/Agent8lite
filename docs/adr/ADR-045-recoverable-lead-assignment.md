# ADR-045 — Recoverable Lead assignment

Integration review, 2026-10-13: fresh Lead create, assignment and stage-transition responses snapshot the exact locked row after their own synchronous activity scoring and audit effects, before persisting the command receipt. Generated validation and exact tenant/resource/source/version checks remain mandatory. Historical receipts, including pre-scoring/null-score receipts, replay unchanged; later activity is reflected by an independent current read. The capture acknowledgement accepts a generated-contract-valid coherent score/version pair or its legacy unscored pair and retains every original command/context/ETag check.

Accepted by the coordinating owner on 2026-10-11 after ADR-044 verification. This bounded M2 slice advances MOD-D-001/003/007, MOD-D-AC-2/3, FL-02, INV-01/02/03, MOD-K-006/007 and the capture → assignment → follow-up exit path. It covers the complete frozen `PATCH /v1/orgs/{orgId}/leads/{leadId}` operation and a recoverable assignment editor. Broader Lead detail editing, reminders, and browser/device acceptance remain separate client gates.

Preserve the closed `LeadPatch` contract: optional `ownerMemberId`, `intent` and `timeline`; required `Idempotency-Key`, CSRF and `If-Match`; `crm.leads.write`; and `200 Lead` with a strong ETag. No query parameters are accepted. Preserve the legacy canonical request hash over the detached body and the exact supplied `If-Match` spelling, including quoted, bare and leading-zero revisions, so existing unexpired receipts remain replayable. A syntactically valid original revision must be in the supported positive integer range; successful execution returns original revision plus one.

The API owns one dedicated Lead-patch command boundary. Before any wait it detaches and validates the queryless route, closed body, printable key, original revision and normalized domain values. It then serializes by tenant, actor, route and key and inspects the matching receipt even when expired. A changed canonical hash conflicts. A matching expired receipt returns `receipt_expired` without execution, renewal or replacement. Replay strictly validates the stored Lead response, command identity, next version, response reference when present and strong ETag. A legacy matching receipt with a null ETag is accepted only after the same historical validation.

Fresh execution and replay require the actual runtime role, matching transaction tenant/actor, active membership/role/organization, exact complete current grants and current CRM capability. Lock and recheck the Lead and its unarchived Contact. The Lead's Contact and source remain immutable. Authorize both the historical receipt owner and the current owner before returning a replay because the historical response discloses its owner. Disabled, foreign, inaccessible or office-ineligible current or historical owners deny replay. For fresh execution, an omitted or normalized-unchanged owner still requires that current owner to remain eligible. An authorized manager may explicitly reassign a Lead away from a disabled current owner after proving current write scope through the locked Lead and office sources; the effective target must be active and eligible. Normalize UUIDs before comparisons so case-only spelling cannot create an assignment.

Own scope may retain or assign the actor only. Team scope may use active members in the actor's currently authorized offices. Organization scope may use any active member in the organization and is the only scope allowed to set `ownerMemberId` to null. Lock the actor, current/historical/target owners and every office relationship used for authorization, then repeat full authorization after each observable wait. Use database time at the final boundary. Revocation, capability/grant change, Contact archival, Lead reassignment, source substitution or office movement denies without partial effects.

Fresh execution performs one compare-and-swap Lead update and one audit record in the caller transaction. If the normalized owner actually changes, it also appends one validated database-time `lead.assigned` outbox event and one privacy-safe Lead activity row containing only resource/member references and the resulting version. An unchanged owner, intent/timeline-only edit or empty patch creates neither assignment event nor assignment activity. No approved Lead-metadata event exists, so those mutations remain atomic with their audit and receipt but have no fabricated outbox event; adding a versioned metadata event is a separate contract proposal. The Lead change, optional assignment event/activity, audit and receipt commit or roll back together.

The assignment editor captures the exact validated `LeadPatch` body, UUID key, original strong ETag and verified user/organization/member/CSRF/access-revision/full-grants binding before sending. It offers assign-to-self and, when authorized, active member choices; organization-scoped users may choose the unassigned queue. Member discovery improves the picker but never becomes mutation authority. The server remains authoritative when a displayed member is stale or outside write scope.

An ambiguous outcome retains that immutable command and blocks competing assignment commands, route changes, organization switch, sign-out, close and service-worker activation. Explicit reconciliation resends the exact body, key and original `If-Match` for at most 23 hours under both wall and monotonic clocks. A strict matching `200 Lead`, expected next-version ETag and requested-field receipt acknowledges the historical assignment. A separate authorized current refresh updates the Lead detail. If a successful assignment moves the Lead outside the actor's current scope, the acknowledgement remains successful while the current view becomes unavailable and clears private detail. Initial rejection remains distinct from an unknown outcome.

ADR-042 local forgetting extends to an expired or clock-invalid unknown Lead assignment with the exact action **Clear this tab’s recovery data**. It sends no request, removes all private command and draft values, invalidates callbacks, releases lifecycle guards, retains a generic warning and blocks another assignment through the same controller binding. A context replacement or document reload may permit a human attempt and cannot prevent a possible duplicate. Offline, hidden and generic recovery states withhold Lead, Contact and member details.

Backend owns the dedicated command/receipt boundary and service path under `apps/api/**`; frontend owns protocol/panel/workspace integration under `apps/web/**`; tester owns PostgreSQL/RLS, protocol/controller/static and integrated acceptance under `tests/**`. Root owns this ADR and integration review. The generic Lead update route must fail closed once the dedicated route is registered. No migration, dependency, public-contract amendment or new event type is approved.

Acceptance covers own/team/organization and tenant isolation; unassignment; disabled/foreign recipients; exact optional-field preservation; UUID normalization; same-key concurrency; distinct-key compare-and-swap races; changed, expired, legacy and corrupt receipts; historical replay after later edits; revision bounds; actual advisory/Lead/Contact/member/office waits; revocation and CRM expiry; rollback; one validated assignment event/activity/audit per actual assignment; and zero assignment events for metadata-only edits. Client acceptance covers exact immutable resend, strict acknowledgement versus current refresh, assignment-away scope loss, initial rejection versus unknown outcome, expiry/clocks/forgetting, stale callbacks, context cuts, navigation/update protection and private-safe rendering. A fresh-database integrated flow proves manager capture, genuine assignment to Agent, Agent linked Task creation/completion and stage transition with scope and activity history. Retain current capture, Contact edit, stage-transition and Task-create behavior. Browser/axe/keyboard and physical installed-mode behavior remain open.
