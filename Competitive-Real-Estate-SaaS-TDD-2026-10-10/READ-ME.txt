10 October 2026

Open real-estate-saas-tdd-2026-10-10.html in a browser. Keep companion folders beside it.

This is a design blueprint, not an implemented or deployed application.
Owner-selected MLS provider: CoreLogic/Cotality Trestle. Authorized Las Vegas account/feed rights and credentials remain M6 inputs. No live feed was connected.
Owner requirement: complete web application and home-screen PWA on iPhone and Android, without app-store distribution. Real-device acceptance is M8.

FILES
- technical-design.json: structured design, modules, requirements, workstreams, milestones and section content.
- contracts/openapi.json: proposed OpenAPI 3.1.1 contract.
- contracts/mls-adapter.ts: type-only provider interface; no actual adapter implementation.
- contracts/events.schema.json and permission-catalog.json: shared boundary contracts.
- contracts/pwa-manifest.example.json and pwa-policy.json: manifest/cache/update design; icons/service worker not implemented.
- data/schema.sql, data-dictionary.json, schema.mmd: proposed PostgreSQL/PostGIS model. SQL not applied.
- design-system: original shared tokens.
- fixtures/synthetic-listings.json: 12 entirely fictional records, fixed test clock, not MLS data.
- future-repo/AGENTS.md: future implementation rules; not installed as this workspace's root instructions.
- reference/source-audit.json: entire previous audit.
- reference/Agent-Elite-Audit-2026-10-10.zip: original audit report and evidence; extract separately to inspect evidence paths.
- diagrams: original architecture, relationships, MLS and workspace design SVGs.
- validation.json, manifest.json: artifact checks and SHA-256 inventory.

REPRODUCIBILITY
Builder source is under source/. With a compatible Node.js runtime, run node source/build.mjs from the extracted folder. It needs no third-party packages, no credentials and no provider calls. It rebuilds design artifacts only. ZIP packaging/physical browser preview are separate delivery checks.

VALIDATION LIMITS
Structural checks do not substitute for PostgreSQL migrations, API tests, physical-device functionality, provider licensing or M8 operational readiness. Read the TDD gates before implementation.
